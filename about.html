About Us

Who We Are

SkyHaven Airlines is a student-created sample airline website for our class project.

Our goal is to make booking a flight simple. Passengers register once, then send reservation requests that are saved directly into our Google Sheets database for our staff to review.

Our Mission

To give every traveler safe, comfortable and affordable flights with friendly service.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted airline for local and international travel.

Our Values

Safety, integrity, customer care and teamwork.
